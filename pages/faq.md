## What are the prerequisites for this course?

To get the most out of this course, you should have prior experience with a general understanding of
machine learning (datasets, probability, classifiers, overfitting/underfitting), basic knowledge of
deep learning (backpropagation, CNNs, autoencoders), coding in PyTorch, Docker and command line
basics, and at least 1 year of general programming experience.

## How many people should be in a project group?

2, per the [course information](../README.md#course-information).

## How is the course graded?

Pass/Fail, based on a midterm, a final, and the project report (see
[course information](../README.md#course-information)).

## Can I use ChatGPT, Copilot, or other AI tools for the exercises, the project, or the report?

Yes, but treat them as a tool, not a substitute for understanding. Validate anything an AI tool
produces before relying on it, and write your report answers in your own words, since the report is
meant to describe *your own* project work and decisions.
